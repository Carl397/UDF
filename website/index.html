Election Manifesto · Local Government 2026

A community-centric plan for Cape Town's future

As a non-racial, non-sexist and all-inclusive party, we believe every decision must be community-centric and evidence-based, addressing root causes, redressing historical injustices, and creating meaningful improvements in the social, political and economic conditions of all.

Preamble

Decisions rooted in communities, guided by evidence

Any decision-making process must be community-centric and evidence-based. Decisions must address the root causes of our challenges, taking cognisance of the macro and micro, internal and external environment.

Transformative local government

We further believe that local government must be transformative, aiming to redress historical injustices and create meaningful improvements in social, political and economic conditions for all.

Community empowerment as foundation

Our vision is that every intervention has community empowerment as its foundation. That vision rests on seven pillars, set out across this manifesto and driven by one promise: dignity for all people.

Party leadership

Andhor Grey Marks is ready to serve

President of the United Democratic Front Party. Andhor Grey Marks brings together academic excellence, leadership experience, and a deep understanding of grassroots community realities.

Academic credentials

  • Postgraduate Diploma in Law (Employment & Social Security Law), University of Cape Town
  • MPhil (Master's degree in Philosophy) in Dispute Resolution, University of Cape Town

Professional & leadership experience

  • Retail Operations Manager (JSE-listed retailer)
  • Divisional Training Manager, HR Partner, HR Manager, and HR Executive
  • Board Member, Transitional Municipal Councillor, NGO Director
  • SRC Chairperson, Youth and Community Leader
  • Taxi Driver (Parkwood–Wynberg)

A life shaped by struggle and service

During the 1980s, Marks was detained multiple times, including under Section 29 of the Internal Security Act (No. 74 of 1982), reflecting his commitment during one of South Africa's most difficult periods. He remains strongly forward-looking and focused on the real challenges facing South African citizens today.

His vision is rooted in

  • Dignity for all people
  • Community empowerment
  • Social, economic and administrative justice
  • Safety and security for all
